Standing method

  • Exploration XP follows Feats of Exploration: percentages of party TXP, retainers excluded, divided by players present.
  • Monster XP, treasure XP, story awards, and any other grants are recorded separately.
  • Whether XP is divided equally, by share, or by another method is stated with the award.
  • If a retainer receives a half-share, the share is recorded explicitly.
  • If a total does not match the session summary, a [?] note is left rather than forcing it.
