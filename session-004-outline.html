1. Room 7 and the crawlspace

  • The party is in room 7 on the 2026-11-08 undercroft plan. The collapse that blocks part of the room is earthquake damage; Anglad, a miner before he was a fighter, works it out.
  • Elowyn notices a crawlspace about 2 ft high where the western wall meets the ceiling.
  • Ed volunteers. A rope is tied to his leg for extraction and he is hoisted into the opening.
  • His infravision shows nothing at first: a cold, damp passage. He crawls about 40 ft in all, and at the edge of his vision is either a wall or another chamber.
  • He scoots back, warns the others that there is a chamber ahead, then goes forward again.
  • While he is in the passage, Anglad sees torchlight from the southern hall. Bozz takes the rope and Anglad keeps watch. Elowyn waits at the blocked door.
  • Anglad and Elowyn hear several people climbing stairs. They speak the local dialect, and Anglad cannot tell whether they are heavily armoured.

2. The visitors

  • The newcomers are surprised to find the party. The first through is a local H'Zhari man with a torch, a leather jacket over an armoured vest, and a short sword. He is shocked to see Anglad and holds up his sword.
  • Anglad asks Elowyn whether to engage or parlay. Elowyn chooses parlay.
  • Anglad calls out that the party is looking for a boy, not trouble.
  • The man points at the party and asks about the dead Amnesiac. Anglad says it was self-defence. The talk turns to candles.
  • They speak in their own dialect. The party catches only hints about the candleman. In the trade tongue they ask what the candleman carried, and name one thing in particular: a leather medallion.
  • The man offers to trade gold for a medallion. Elowyn wants it, and the party says it does not have it.
  • Anglad is unsure, asks the party, then calls out more names to bluff about how many people are in the room.
  • The visitors' morale check, not the party's, is called for, and they back beyond the corner and move away.
  • Ed returns from the crawlspace.

3. The domed chamber

  • Ed checks the candle medallion for magic and finds nothing. He reads the wand instead: it is fading, 1 to 9 charges.
  • Elowyn enters the crawlspace with a rope around his ankle. He senses a slight downward grade, about 20 degrees.
  • Anglad hears no more footsteps.
  • Elowyn reaches the end of the passage and looks out at the top of an enormous circular chamber with a domed ceiling. A narrow stair spirals down to the floor. It does not look like a well. The air is dead and stale. He crawls back.
  • Bozz holds the rope. Anglad watches the path.
  • Elowyn reports what he saw. Anglad suggests Elowyn scout ahead for an ambush. The party rests first, and agrees to come back and look at the domed chamber later.
  • Dio says the people from earlier were looking for the man, and that one of them said, "that's probably the man." Dio also heard them say they were looking for a Chandler.
  • Ed puts the medallion away.

4. The rough tunnels and the five

  • Elowyn scouts the hall. The party follows 20 ft behind.
  • The passage ends at a stairwell down 30 ft. At the bottom the corridor takes a sharp right. Elowyn listens at the corner and hears nothing, and the party follows.
  • An entrance immediately opposite the stairwell leads south. The corridor continues west for another 30 ft. These tunnels are far more roughly hewn than the rooms above.
  • Elowyn sneaks ahead into the southern room. Beyond an archway a 10 ft stairwell leads down and opens to the left into a chamber 10 ft wide and 40 ft long.
  • Five people are waiting in the darkness. The H'Zhari man from the parlay is among them. They rush Elowyn, and he escapes back up the stairs.

5. The stairwell fight

  • Round 1, the party wins initiative. Ed fires the wand for 7 damage, hitting one in the chest and dropping him; the wand can spend two charges in a round. Elowyn throws down caltrops. Bozz blocks the hallway. Anglad sets his spear behind Bozz for the first charge.
  • The enemy morale roll is 7. They hold despite the first casualty.
  • Two of the enemy at the back step on the caltrops; the other two come on cleanly.
  • One charges up the stairs at Bozz and narrowly avoids Anglad's set spear. The attack scrapes off Bozz's armour. Another swings at Bozz and goes wide.
  • Round 2, the party wins initiative. Elowyn's arrow hits one in the face for 1 damage. Bozz goes wide with his mace. Anglad fumbles his spear.
  • At the front, one blow is deflected by Bozz's shield. Another is a natural 20, and Bozz splinters the shield to block it.
  • Round 3, the party loses initiative. The two behind hobble up but are blocked by the two in front. Bozz is attacked twice and both miss.
  • Anglad moves beside Bozz. Ed fires two wand charges for 3 damage each. Elowyn's arrow hits for 4 and finishes a combatant. Bozz's swing sends a body down the stairs. Anglad's spear takes another through the face and head.
  • Round 4, the last combatant fails morale and turns to retreat. Bozz's mace does 3 damage and knocks him back. Anglad uses the heft of his spear to knock him unconscious.
  • Combat ends with one prisoner. The H'Zhari man from the parlay was among the five and is among the dead; the prisoner is a different man.

6. The prisoner and the puddles

  • The room is older than the tunnels that lead into it. There is a door on the east wall, blocked by collapse, a corridor south, and a door after 20 ft, with a corridor beyond it.
  • Elowyn collects his caltrops. Anglad takes the coin and the four medallions from the four dead: 11 gp, 20 sp, 38 cp, and four leather medallions stamped with a dagger coming out of a book. The four leather tunics or suits of armour, four short swords and two torches are left at the stairwell.
  • Bozz and Anglad tie the prisoner, with a rope to his hand and neck. He is slapped awake.
  • He says Mazarak sent them down to look for a Chandler, because Mazarak believes there is a hoard down here.
  • He points west to the well. He says the literators roped down it, and that he knows little about the Danarose boy and thinks the monster has him.
  • He tells them about the sorceress, the Collector.
  • While he is being questioned, Ed sees translucent things sliding slowly across the floor. They reach the blood from the fight and feed. The notes call them slimes.
  • The party decides to let the prisoner lead them to the well.

7. The junction and the rats

  • The tunnel has two corridors, north and south, and a large door at the end.
  • The prisoner says the north tunnel loops around, and that the south tunnel was not explored because it felt wrong: demon worshippers, some kind of temple.
  • Scurrying comes from the cracks in the walls. Seven rats, large and pig-like, come out. They fail to surprise the party.
  • Round 1, the party loses initiative. Two rats charge the prisoner, one hits for 3 damage. Two charge Anglad, one hits for 3 damage and the other criticals for 5. Two charge Bozz and both miss.
  • Elowyn's bow shot critically fails and hits Bozz; the arrow bounces off his armour and does no damage.
  • Ed throws a ration into the fray and leads three rats away.
  • Bozz crushes a rat. Anglad cannot hit any. The prisoner kicks one of the rats.
  • Round 2, the party wins initiative. Anglad still cannot hit. Elowyn slays a rat attached to Anglad. Ed just misses. Bozz kills one of the rats chewing on the prisoner.
  • The rats fail morale and the survivors flee into the cracks in the walls. The fight ends.
  • What the party does with the prisoner and the medallions is unknown until the next session.
