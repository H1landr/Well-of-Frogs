Combat

HR-01Shields Shall Be Splintered

A character currently using a shield may destroy it to block all damage from an attack.

The 2026 OSE player's book states in each class entry whether the class is martial, semi-martial or non-martial, and that label governs here (DM ruling, 2026-11-07).

  • Martial classes may declare the block after the attacker rolls damage.
  • Semi-martial classes with access to shields may declare the block before damage is rolled.
  • In either case the shield is completely destroyed and unusable afterward; it can be repaired under HR-04.
  • Normal shields can block only damage from a physical attack.
  • Damage from traps, the environment, or poison cannot be blocked by a normal shield.
  • Certain magical shields can block magical attacks.

HR-02Always Wear Your Helmet

  • Classes that can equip shields can also equip helmets.
  • A helmet does not provide an AC bonus.
  • A critical hit is any natural 20 on an attack roll (DM ruling, 2026-11-07).
  • A helmet can absorb the damage from a single critical hit, then is rendered useless.
  • Wearing a helmet imposes a -1 penalty to Surprise.

HR-03Splash and Area Attack Deviation

Added at the table on 2026-11-01. When a splash or area attack misses, it deviates from the target.

  • Distance: it deviates 1 ft for every point by which the attack roll missed.
  • Direction: roll a d12.
d12Where it lands
1Bounces back, towards the thrower
2Falls short, somewhere in between
3-6Goes wide; roll a second d6, 1-3 left, 4-6 right
7-12Too far, past the target

A wide throw takes a second roll: d6, where 1-3 puts it to the left and 4-6 to the right.

HR-04Repairing Destroyed Shields and Helmets

A shield destroyed by HR-01 or a helmet destroyed by HR-02 can be repaired rather than replaced (DM ruling, 2026-11-07). The repair takes one of two forms:

  • Paid repair: it costs the same as buying a new one.
  • Armorer: a character with the Armorer secondary skill and access to materials can repair it with one day of work.

The three rules below were added on 2026-11-08 and are grouped with the combat rules.

HR-18Set for Charge

A house rule, added 2026-11-08. A character may set a spear or a polearm against a charge. When an enemy charges the character, or an ally close by, the set weapon may attack the charging enemy during the enemy's movement segment, before the charge's attack, for normal damage.

  • The attack is allowed only if the character's side also won initiative that round.
  • Spears can be set this way, and some polearms can. The referee decides whether a given polearm qualifies.
  • The rule covers a charge against the character or against an ally close enough to be covered by the set weapon.

HR-19Firing into Melee

An optional OSE rule, adopted 2026-11-08. When a missile attack is made into a melee, first determine randomly which figure in the melee is struck, then roll to hit that figure.

  • Every figure in the melee, friend or enemy, is a possible target.
  • The attack is a normal attack against the figure the roll selects.
  • If the attack misses, the missile misses.
  • Because each figure is equally likely, a melee with more enemies than allies is more likely to put an enemy in the line of fire.

HR-20Blindness and Total Darkness

An optional OSE rule, adopted 2026-11-08. A character who cannot see, whether in total darkness or through another cause of blindness, suffers:

  • -4 to attack rolls.
  • -4 to saving throws.
  • -4 to AC (AC worsens by 4).

The attack penalty is the usual one for total darkness. The saving throw and AC penalties are added to it.

HR-06Fighter: Favored Weapon Type

At 1st level a fighter may choose a weapon type preferred over all others. The fighter receives +1 to hit and +1 to damage with that weapon type in combat. That is the fighter's free specialisation under HR-09.

HR-07Cleric: Sanctified Weapons

At character creation a cleric chooses three weapon types as the weapons of their god. These weapons may be used without penalty. Other weapons may not be used. The choices should make sense for the deity.

HR-08Ranger: Monster Slayer

A ranger receives +1 to attack and damage rolls against giants and monstrous creatures larger than a man.

HR-09Weapon Proficiencies

An optional OSE rule, adopted on the DM's instruction on 2026-10-27. Proficiencies are declared at 1st level.

AptitudeAt 1st levelThereafterNon-proficient penalty
Martial4One every 3 levels-2
Semi-martial3One every 4 levels-3
Non-martial1One every 5 levels-5

Weapon specialisation (+1 attack and damage with one weapon) is open to martial characters only.

Martial classes: one specialisation is free, and a second costs one weapon proficiency slot (DM rulings, 2026-11-05). For a fighter the free one is HR-06's favoured weapon. Rangers are martial and qualify; semi-martial and non-martial characters do not.

Exploration and equipment

HR-10Moving and Encumbrance, Item-Based

Use the item-based encumbrance system from Carcass Crawler #2 (Gavin Norman, pp. 28-29).

  • Track significant items rather than coin weight.
  • Classify each item as equipped or packed.
  • Look up both totals and use the slower movement rate.

The full procedure, movement table, and gear weights are on the encumbrance reference page.

Classes and advancement

HR-12Magic-User: Starting Spells

A newly created magic-user begins with four random Level 1 spells in their spellbook, in addition to Read Magic.

HR-13Thief: D6 Skills

Use the D6 thief skills from Carcass Crawler #1.

  • All skills start at 1-in-6.
  • A 1st-level thief allocates 4 expertise points; each point raises one skill by 1-in-6.
  • +2 points per level thereafter, to a maximum of 5-in-6 per skill.
  • Read languages is available from 1st level and can be improved, per the article. The same article's later section still repeats the standard "from 4th level" wording; this campaign follows the 1st-level version.

HR-14Thief Hit Die and Starting HP

A table clarification rather than part of the v1.0 PDF.

  • Thief hit die: d6 (the 2026 OSE DM option).
  • Starting HP: maximum hit die result plus the CON modifier.
  • Example: a Thief 1 with CON 8 (-1): 6 - 1 = 5 HP.

The maximum-HP-at-1st-level rule applies to PCs only. Replacement characters get it as well; newly recruited retainers do not (DM ruling, 2026-11-07).

Magic and ancestry

HR-15Detect Magic and Read Magic

Detect Magic. All classes capable of arcane magic can detect magical resonance at will if they can concentrate without distraction and physically touch the subject.

  • Only the existence of magic is detected.
  • The nature and strength of the magic are not revealed.
  • Each attempt requires one turn.

Read Magic. Read Magic is not necessary to decipher scrolls. It is necessary to decipher spellbooks.

HR-16Trollkin: Increased Healing

A Draga'Kyr, or Trollkin, heals faster than other ancestries:

  • An 8-hour rest heals d3 HP.
  • A 24-hour rest heals 3d3 HP.

HR-17Reading Magic Item Charges

A character of a class capable of arcane magic may concentrate for one turn to learn a rough estimate of how many charges remain in a magic item. The reading falls into one of four tiers.

TierCharges left
Fading1-9, a single digit
Steady10-50, the lower double digits
Radiant51-99, the higher double digits
Blinding100 or more

An exact count requires a higher-level divination spell, or a person knowledgeable in the creation of magic items (DM rule, 2026-11-07).
