Party Funds

What the party holds in common, what it has been paid, and what is still owed. Coins in a character's own purse are not tracked here or anywhere.

active updated 2026-11-01 6 holdings 10 payments

Still outstanding

  • The chest of treasure. Promised on delivery of Orlando, uncounted, and not taken, because Orlando has not been delivered.
  • The scrolls. Traded rather than sold, but a trade counts as a sale for experience. The 500 XP is inside the Session 3 award, not on top of it.
  • Dio's share. None recorded. As a hireling he takes no share of exploration XP, and whether he sees treasure XP is unsettled.

How it is kept

  • Recovered value and liquidated value are kept apart.
  • The ledger records who is carrying what.
  • Shares for PCs, retainers, and faction obligations are noted where they exist.
  • Treasure XP is linked to the experience ledger when it is awarded.
  • The payment sequence was confirmed on 2026-10-21: Wakhar offered 5 gp each, the fee rose by 10 gp each once he surrendered, and Kollaro paid 15 gp each.
